JSON schema identifiers and their versioning policy

Machine-readable reports carry a `schema` key `polytool.<name>.v<N>`. Within a
version, keys are only added, never removed or renamed; a renamed key keeps
being emitted under its old name next to the new one. A breaking change bumps
the version. Simple per-polynomial reports (real-rooted, interlacing,
properties, gamma-expansion, ...) have the shape {"items": [...]} without a
schema key.

In polytool.bkw-certification.v1, the circle criterion gives result proved with
empty conditional_on. Its circle.provenance is exactly:
  circle criterion for Hessenberg Toeplitz symbols (unpublished manuscript)
A real limit spectrum alone remains conditional under
hessenberg_real_limit_spectrum, recorded in conditional_on.
