Discover and replay general recurrence proofs.

Discover general proofs for a recurrence and seeds; no named-sequence lookup.
Tries, in the order of the parent cascade, product induction, Favard/three-term,
Liu-Wang, derivative-lag, Ma-Wang interval, Liu-Wang three-term, iterated
preserver and hypergeometric-row criteria, then the RealRooted row routes
fixed-delay window (variable_delay: f(n) P_n = A(n) P_(n-1) + B(n) x P_(n-L)
with a delay L of at least 2) and residue classes (residue_class_subsequences:
every lag a multiple of a period p of at least 2, each class P_(first + j + p m)
proved by a criterion above), the last two recorded only when the rule has their
form; every hypothesis is checked exactly for all indices. Reports every
attempt, its obligations and conclusion. A fit still needs an all-index identity
for an independently defined family. Rust certificates can be replayed; Lean
replay is not attempted.

Iterated preserver also searches bounded exact factorizations of lag-one
second-order steps over Q(n)[x]. Each first-order factor uses the existing
preserver menu or the shared Liu-Wang conditions on its actual input degree. The
certificate records application order and replays the full Ore identity;
intermediate degree and sign conditions hold for every rule index. This can
certify a closed ray even when the composite fails a whole-line preserver test.
No simplicity or interlacing is inferred from the factorization alone.

Index-section lift (recorded only when it applies): the rows, possibly reversed,
are every p-th row (p <= 5) of a constant-coefficient parent found by factoring,
an identity for every index; the parent is proved by the criteria.

Factor gauge (recorded only when it applies): every lag-j multiplier is
kappa_j(n) q(x)^j for one fixed q, so P_n = q^(n-e) g S_n with S_n in the span
of the gauged seed rows; S_n is real-rooted for every n by its degree (<= 1), a
discriminant (degree 2 or 3) or the palindromic-quartic test (u = x + 1/x),
signs of polynomials in n decided exactly.

Positive lag-two Euler (lag_two_euler, recorded only when its form matches):
with theta = x D and m = n - first_index, the operators are [(a+b
theta)+x(a+b(m-1)-b theta)] P_(n-1) and c x(theta+1)(m-1-theta) P_(n-2). The
full operator identity is exact; a > 0, b >= a, c >= 0 and a nonzero denominator
are checked for every index. Positive constant/linear seeds and exact later seed
steps give simple negative roots, adjacent strict interlacing and auxiliary
interlacing, with a replayable certificate and RealRooted
lagTwoEuler_recurrence_certificate correspondence. Negative lag corrections are
excluded. Tried after the existing routes.

Binomial-perturbed Euler orbit (binomial_perturbed_euler_orbit, recorded only
when its operators match): the exact lag-two theta recurrence of RealRooted
issue #2513, s >= 1, kappa >= s+4, Q_0=1, Q_1=1+2u, Q_2=1+kappa*u+kappa*u^2,
starting at degree m >= 3. Integer degree shifts, every seed and nonzero scalar
denominators are checked exactly. Also recognizes Q_m(u)=(1+u)^m P_n(u/(1+u)) by
exact Mobius transport with an inductive degree cap. Gives simple roots in
(-1,0) for Q, or (-inf,0) for P, and strict adjacent interlacing with the
direction preserved. A certificate replays the transformed rule and seed
identities. Lean: strictInterl_of_orbitPerturbation_recurrence.

Rational PF Riordan (rational_pf_riordan, recorded only when it applies): for an
index-free homogeneous rule without derivatives, build the exact reduced row
generating function from every prescribed seed. Recognize F(x,z)=d(z)/(1-x z
d(z) K(z)), with d(0)>0 and nonzero rational PF series d,K. The rational Edrei
criterion is decided by exact gcd layers, PRS root signs and root isolation:
numerator zeros nonpositive, denominator poles positive, positive initial
scalar. Chen--Fu--Ruan Theorems 3(3) and 4 give PF rows for all indices,
including K(0)=0 and noninvertible arrays. Certificates separately replay the
full generating-function identity, Riordan identity, Edrei criterion for d and
for K, TNN product (Cauchy--Binet), and Chen--Fu--Ruan row theorem. Zero rows
are reported exactly; no simplicity or interlacing is claimed. The route is
available to index-section parents and the affine marker shifts.

Shared reversal closure (degree_law_reversal, after direct routes and lifts):
x^(a*m+b) P_(first_index+m)(1/x), with an inductive degree bound from the lag
multipliers and every seed. Try only the minimal admissible slope (zero
allowed), guarded by 3, and minimal seed offset. Skip already tried records (up
to index shift) and invariant shape failures; run eligible nonrecursive theorems
in the reversed coordinate. The Riordan GF is computed once; extending the seed
prefix with generated rows cannot change it. The transform, certificate shape
and replay are shared with index sections. Reciprocal nonzero roots and possible
extra roots at 0; zero rows preserved. The text report prints the named theorem
chain and each closure separately.

When no criterion proves the record:
  - tail: if one of the first rows is zero or not real-rooted, the criteria run
    on the record starting after the last such row; a proof then says "every
    row from index N on is real-rooted" (real_rooted_from_index);
  - alternative representations: the recurrence finder's menu on generated rows
    proposes other rules (first-order derivative rules with a factor f(n), lag
    at most two); a rule is used only after an all-index equality certificate
    (Ore-algebra division and invariant row annihilators, exact), then the
    criteria run on it. --max-representation-candidates bounds each finder pass
    (deterministic count; 0 disables the stage);
  - catalog lifts: the record is connected to a family of the bundled OEIS
    catalog by an identity certified for every index: a normalized
    identification T_n = c(n) x^(a n + b) S_(n+s) (candidates from a
    fingerprint index of the catalog rows, the scalar proved nonzero), or a
    backward basis-map lift Q_n = T_P(R_n) whose map theorem carries the roots
    of R into a target interval. The parent (S, or R) is proved by the stages
    above (never by another lift); R may also be identified with a catalog
    family. When the parent is only flagged lean_proved in the catalog (a proof
    made outside polytool), the status is conditional_on_attestation, never
    proved. --max-lift-candidates bounds the catalog families certified
    (deterministic order; 0 disables the stage). Not run when a root region is
    requested;
  - affine coordinates: when still nothing proves the record, the criteria run
    on it in exact coordinates x = a u + b (Q_n(u) = P_n(a u + b): every
    coefficient substituted, D_u = a D_x on derivative terms, the seeds
    mapped), with b a rational root of a factor free of n of a coefficient (a
    = 1, then a = -1) and the reflection x = -u. A proof maps back: same rows
    nonzero and real-rooted, root region a R + b (ends swapped for a < 0),
    interlacing kept for a > 0 and mirrored for a < 0. No other stage runs in
    the new coordinate. --max-affine-coordinates bounds the coordinates tried
    (deterministic menu; 0 disables the stage).
  - structural closures: after all earlier routes fail, compute coefficient
    weight quotients, row-factor cofactors, inverse gamma expansions, and
    preimages under 1 +/- D or theta + a (a = 1, 2). Prove computed parents
    through the registry, closure depth two, with no parent catalog lookup or
    attestations. Exact Ore identities and named closure chains are required.
    --max-lift-candidates bounds retained candidates per structural stage
    (capped at 6; 0 disables). A requested root region skips this stage.
A rule with an inhomogeneous term matches no criterion: status not_applicable.

The recurrence input is recurrence.v1 JSON, a theorem wrapper or a
representation menu; with a menu, --candidate is required unless it has one
entry. --verify replays the recorded routes of a report from standard input
(every attempt, the tail, every equality certificate and the catalog-lift
certificates with their parent reports, the affine coordinates with their
transformed records), and structural closure chains; takes no search flags.

Usage: polytool recurrence-prove [--recurrence <FILE|->] [--candidate N] [--max-start-delay N] [--max-representation-candidates N] [--max-lift-candidates N] [--max-affine-coordinates N]
       polytool recurrence-prove --verify < proof.json

Options:
      --recurrence <FILE|->
          Read recurrence JSON from FILE (default -: stdin or --input FILE)
      --candidate <N>
          Explicit 0-based menu choice
      --max-start-delay <N>
          Induction-start retries (maximum 16) [default: 3]
      --max-representation-candidates <N>
          Finder configurations per alternative-representation pass (0: off) [default: 300]
      --max-lift-candidates <N>
          Catalog/structural lift candidates (0: off; structural cap 6) [default: 6]
      --max-affine-coordinates <N>
          Coordinates x = a u + b tried by the affine-coordinate stage (0: off) [default: 8]
      --verify
          Replay the recorded routes of a report from stdin; no search flags
  -h, --help
          Print help

Input and output:
      --input <FILE>     Read the input from FILE instead of stdin (`-` is stdin)
      --format <FORMAT>  Output format [default: text] [possible values: text, json]
      --json             Emit machine-readable JSON (same as --format json)

Input:
  A JSON document on stdin (or --input FILE), described on this page.

Details:
  After every proof route fails, stationary recurrences receive a separate exact
  BKW/row refutation stage. Status refuted carries bkw_refutation with a
  replayable certificate. A real limit set alone never supplies a proof; a
  refutation never replaces a successful proof. See bkw certify for public proofs
  and explicitly named conditional all-row claims.

  JSON output is polytool.recurrence-proof-discovery.v1. Its trivially_real_rooted
  is true when the supplied (not fitted) rule is B(n) P_n = A(x,n) P_(n-k) and
  product induction proves it. trivially_real_rooted_if_rule_holds classifies the
  rule conditional on its holding for all indices, including fitted rules.
  real_rooted_from_index is the first index N with every row P_n, n >= N, proved
  nonzero and real-rooted. A residue-class proof may leave whole residue classes
  of zero rows (conclusion.zero_residue_classes, residues of n modulo the period);
  real_rooted_from_index is then omitted and
  real_rooted_outside_zero_residue_classes gives from_index and the zero classes
  (status proved). tail and alternative_representations hold the later stages,
  each alternative with its polytool.recurrence-equality.v1 certificate.
  catalog_lifts holds the catalog-lift stage: identifications (catalog id,
  normalized-equality certificate, the parent's own discovery report, the
  transfer), basis_maps (the backward basis-map search and coefficient
  identifications) and conclusion (route, catalog_family or basis_map,
  parent_proof polytool or catalog_attestation, conditional_on, from_index,
  root_region). Status conditional_on_attestation: the only lift rests on a
  catalog flag; real_rooted_from_index is then omitted and the claim is in
  catalog_lifts.conclusion. affine_coordinates holds the affine-coordinate stage:
  candidates_available, coordinates (slope, intercept, reason, the transformed
  record and its attempts, a transformed tail) and conclusion (coordinate,
  theorem, from_index, root_region mapped to x, interlacing with
  interlacing_orientation preserved or mirrored). closure_lifts holds structural
  inverse operations, all-index certificates, computed parent proofs (registry or
  recursive closure), named steps and the nonzero-row conclusion. Replay needs the
  attempts of exactly the criteria the current search records; recompute reports
  saved before a criterion was added.

Examples:
  polytool recurrence --json < rows.txt | polytool recurrence-prove --recurrence - --json
  polytool recurrence-prove --recurrence rec.json --json > proof.json
    polytool recurrence-prove --verify < proof.json

Exit status:
  0  proved by polytool (or the replayed report is valid)
  1  not proved by polytool (also conditional_on_attestation)
  2  usage error, invalid input or a failed replay
